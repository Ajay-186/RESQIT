Current emergency response systems are plagued by delayed reporting, poor inter-agency coordination, lack of real-time visibility, and inefficient responder allocation.

🚑

Delayed Emergency Response

Inaccurate Location Sharing

Panicked or injured citizens cannot give landmarks or street names accurately over voice calls, sending responders to wrong blocks.

Duplicate Incident Reports

A single highway crash sparks dozens of separate emergency calls, overwhelming call center operators and wasting scarce emergency fleets.

No Centralized Monitoring

⏱

Increased Response Time
